Orbital Forge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Orbital Forge ("the game") is published by Pixel Snap Games. This policy explains what the game stores, why, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account data. If you create an account, your email address is stored by Firebase Authentication (Google LLC), which also handles your password securely. We use your email to sign you in, to send a verification/welcome email when you sign up, and to send password-reset emails you ask for. Guest accounts store only a random identifier.
  • Game progress. Your save (planets, resources, currencies, settings) is stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore under your account so you can continue on another device.
  • Purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play. We record the product and order reference so purchases can be credited and restored. We never see your payment details.
  • Ads. The game shows optional rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one. These are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID and usage data to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses data from apps. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent first.
  • Device storage. A copy of your save is kept on your device so the game works offline.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal data, and we don't collect your location, contacts, photos or files.

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings → Account → Delete account and data. This removes your account and cloud save immediately. Or see delete your account to request it by email.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

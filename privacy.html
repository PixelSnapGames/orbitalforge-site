Orbital Forge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Orbital Forge ("the game") is published by Pixel Snap Games. This policy explains what the game stores, why, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account data. If you create an account, your email address is stored by Firebase Authentication (Google LLC), which also handles your password securely. If you sign in with Google, we receive the email address and display name of your Google account from Google Sign-In and store the email address. We use your email to sign you in, to send a verification/welcome email when you sign up with a password, and to send password-reset emails you ask for. Guest accounts store only a random identifier.
  • Game progress. Your save (planets, resources, currencies, settings) is stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore under your account so you can continue on another device.
  • Purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play. We record the product and order reference so purchases can be credited and restored. We never see your payment details.
  • Ads. The game shows optional rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one. These are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID and usage data to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses data from apps. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent first.
  • Leaderboards. If you are signed in (including as a guest) and choose a callsign, your callsign, Commander Level, XP, galaxies sold, lifetime credits and best weekly galaxy value are shown to other players on the in-game leaderboards. Callsigns are picked from preset word lists. Your email is never shown.
  • Display name. If you sign in with Google or a confirmed email account, you can choose a display name (since version 1.13). It is stored with your account, shown to other players on the leaderboards instead of your callsign, and can be changed once a month. Please don't use your real name. Names must follow the community rules; names that break them can be reset and blocked by us.
  • Reports. If you report another player's display name, we store the name, the reason you chose, your account ID and the time, so we can review it. Only we can read reports.
  • Messages and gifts. The game downloads news, events, sales and gifts we publish. To see how a message does, the game adds one to a shared counter when it is shown, when its button is tapped and when its gift is claimed; these counters contain no identifier. Messages we send to one player are stored under that player's account, with whether they were read and whether the gift was claimed.
  • Play statistics. While you play, the game sends an anonymous daily summary to Firebase Cloud Firestore: a random install identifier created on your device, the date you first played, the app version, the minutes you played that day, how many times you opened the game, your Commander Level, your furthest star system, your total play time and whether you are signed in. It contains no name, email address or account ID, and we use it only to see how the game is played and improve it. In the Android app (since version 1.17), Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) also receives a few anonymous events about how the game is played: levels reached, research done, planets and star systems unlocked, Ascend, rewarded ads watched, the tutorial, the automation priority, and which screens are opened. Google adds a random app-instance identifier and basic device information (such as the phone model, Android version and the country, estimated from your IP address). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can turn all play statistics off at any time in Settings → Game → Share anonymous play stats.
  • Feedback. If you answer the in-game feedback questions, your star rating and answers are stored with the same random install identifier, the app version, your Commander Level and total play time. Please don't include personal information in your answers.
  • Device storage. A copy of your save is kept on your device so the game works offline.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal data. The game itself doesn't collect your precise location, contacts, photos or files (Google AdMob may estimate your general region from your IP address to serve ads, as described above).

Deleting your data

In the game: tap your insignia (top right) → Settings → Account → Delete account and data. This removes your account, cloud save, display name, leaderboard entries and personal messages immediately. Or see delete your account to request it by email.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

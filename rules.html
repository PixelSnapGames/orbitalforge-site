Orbital Forge – Community Rules

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Orbital Forge lets signed-in players choose a display name that other players see on the leaderboards. These rules apply to every display name. You agree to them in the game before saving your first name.

Display names must not

  • be offensive, hateful, harassing, sexual or violent;
  • target a person or a group;
  • pretend to be another player, a real person, Pixel Snap Games or the developer;
  • contain ads, links or contact details.

The game also refuses names with common offensive words and reserved words before they are saved.

Reporting a name

On the leaderboards, tap ⋯ next to a display name and choose Report this name. You can also hide a name on your own phone from the same menu; that player's callsign shows instead. Or email pixelsnapgames@gmail.com.

What we do

We review every report. A name that breaks these rules is reset and blocked so it can't be used again, and we may remove that player's leaderboard entries. The player can then choose a new name that follows the rules.

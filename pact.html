The rules

  1. Games only, in English. Your game is in Google Play closed testing.
  2. Install every game on the roster and stay opted in until the last game finishes its 14 days. No leaving once your own test passes.
  3. Actually play. Open each game a few times a week and try to make progress.
  4. Leave feedback on every game at least once, right here on its card.
  5. Inactive members get removed and replaced, so the Pact stays strong for everyone.

With 20 games, plan on 20 to 30 minutes a day.

Test everyone, then prove it

Once you're approved, open the Games tab. For each game tap Opt in, then Install, and tick it off. Play them, and leave feedback on each one.

When you've opted in to all of them, upload screenshots in the Me tab. The organizer checks them and marks you Verified.

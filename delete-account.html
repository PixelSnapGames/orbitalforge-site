Delete your Orbital Forge account

Fastest way, inside the game:

  1. Open Orbital Forge and tap your insignia (the hexagon with your level, top right), then Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and data and confirm.

Your account, email address, cloud save and leaderboard entries are deleted immediately.

Can't open the game?

Email pixelsnapgames@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Orbital Forge account". We delete the account and all its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email address and password, or the link to your Google account)
  • Your cloud save and game progress
  • Your leaderboard entries (callsign, level and scores) and your display name
  • Messages and gifts we sent to your account
  • Your purchase records linked to the account (Google Play keeps its own payment records as required by law)
